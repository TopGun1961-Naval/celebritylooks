About CelebrityLooks

What celebrities wear, who made it, and how to get the look.

CelebrityLooks.com is a searchable record of what celebrities wear on red carpets, at premieres and on the street, organized by celebrity, event, style and item. Every look lists the designer and pieces as reported by fashion publications or announced by the brands themselves, with links to those sources, plus similar styles you can buy at a range of prices.

How a look gets on the site

  • We start from public reporting: designer announcements, brand press releases and fashion outlets such as Vogue, WWD, Harper’s Bazaar and Elle.
  • Every identification names its sources on the page. When sources disagree or are unclear, we leave the detail out.
  • An editor checks each look before it is marked verified. Looks still waiting for that check carry a yellow “Editor check pending” label and are kept out of search engines.

What we don’t do

  • We never say a celebrity recommends a product or this site. A celebrity was “spotted wearing” an item; everything else is a “similar style.”
  • We don’t publish quotes, rumors or personal news about the people we cover.
  • We don’t use paparazzi, scraped or AI-generated images of real people. Photos are licensed editorial images, brand press images or official social posts, credited on our photo credits page.

Who runs it

CelebrityLooks.com is an independent publication. Research and matching are done with AI tools and checked by a human editor before a look is marked verified. Contact us at editors@celebritylooks.com.
